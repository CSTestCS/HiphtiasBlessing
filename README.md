# Hiphtia's Blessing

A 3D workplace stealth comedy for Windows. Import your own VRoid / VRM characters for yourself, your coworkers, your manager, the townsfolk and the angel Celesti. Then survive work, commutes and weekends after a wardrobe mishap leaves you without pants or a skirt.

## The story
1. **Monday**: wake up, get ready and commute to work dressed normally. During your first task, a wardrobe mishap that fits the task (a printer jam, a spilled pot of coffee, a dish sprayer...) takes your pants or skirt.
2. **Monday night**: you take everything down to the basement laundry room. The machine locks with all your clothes inside, your neighbor comes down to do laundry, and when the door finally opens, your whole wardrobe is ruined. From now on you go everywhere without legwear.
3. **The morning after your third shift**: you wake with a start; Celesti, the angelic messenger of **Hiphtia, goddess of hips**, appears in your apartment. You've been "blessed", and she's very sorry about it. From now on, the coworker you spend the most time near each workday gets blessed the next workday.
4. **Win** when every coworker shares the blessing.

The week runs Monday to Friday at work. Saturdays and Sundays are **days off with errands** around town.

## Main menu
- **Continue / Load Game**: multiple save slots, each its own game. Coworkers from your other saves walk around town as named townsfolk.
- **New Game**: create your character (name, gender, body, legwear, colors or your own .vrm), pick one of 7 jobs, and set up 4–12 coworkers plus a manager.
- **Settings**: length of an in-game hour (default 4 real minutes), shame buildup intensity, facial expression strength, minimap on/off, bundled townsfolk on/off.
- **Civilian Models**: 4 bundled townsperson models are used by default. Import more .vrm templates (with a gender). Every NPC gets random skin, hair, eye and clothing colors.
- **Celesti's Model**: the bundled Celesti model is used by default. Import your own .vrm (a halo and wings are added) and set her name.

Everyone starts on one of the bundled VRoid bodies (young/older, female/male), tinted with the colors you pick. Each morning an **outfit screen** lets you pick everyone's outfit for the day: which outfit, whether it's pants or a skirt today, and top/bottom color tints. You also pick your **at-home outfit** there.

Model options per outfit: which parts are legwear, which parts turn **see-through when wet**, an optional underwear overlay (off by default, since VRoid bodies already have underwear), and **wearing another model's clothes** fitted to the body.

## Jobs
Office Worker, Restaurant Server, Retail Worker, Hotel Staff, Library Assistant, Warehouse Worker and Cafe Barista. Every save generates its own workplace:
- a job-specific public floor (cubicle farm, dining room and bar, sales floor, hotel lobby, library stacks, warehouse racks, cafe)
- a back-of-house wing whose size, side and layout differ per job and per save, split into rooms and branching corridors: locker room with the time clock, manager's office, restrooms, break room, storage, loading dock, plus job rooms such as meeting rooms, kitchens, a walk-in freezer, guest rooms, a laundry, an archive, study rooms or a roastery
- lots of cover: plants, vending machines, carts, cabinets, benches, seats and hiding spots, and things to pick up and carry in front of you
- 15+ task types per job

Each coworker has a role (host, line cook, cashier, housekeeper...) with a home station and the tasks that go with it, and they pick nearby stations instead of crossing the building.

## Rules
- **Shame** rises when people outside work see you and when you're caught at work. Fill it and you lose. Sleeping lowers it. The Settings screen scales how fast it builds.
- **Makeshift skirts**: tie a carried box, newspaper, folder, tote bag, towel or tablecloth on with F. Each fails in its own way: boxes crack, paper blows away, bags tear, cloth snags on chairs. People find it odd, so it still drains Composure.
- **Excuses**: when someone comes over because you're acting strange, pick an excuse ("Posture exercise.", "Workplace ergonomics."...). Whether it works depends on who's asking.
- **Hiding**: carry a box, tray, bag, newspaper, umbrella... in front of you (E to pick up, G to put down), sit at desks, tables and benches (nobody can tell while you're seated), duck into hiding spots, or crouch behind low furniture.
- **Composure** drains when you crouch or run in plain view at work. At zero, someone comes over to check on you.
- **Stamina** limits running.
- **Coworker personalities** when they catch you:
  - *Best Friend*: becomes an ally. Takes turns lending you their legwear (90 seconds at a time, swapped somewhere private) while they sneak around in your place, or causes a distraction.
  - *Sympathetic*: becomes an ally and causes distractions.
  - *Neutral*: lets it slide once, then reports you.
  - *Strict* / *Distant*: report you.
  - *Oddball*: thinks it's a game, slips off somewhere private to take off their own bottoms, then starts sneaking around too.
  - Anyone of the opposite gender may just look away and let it slide (once a day).
- **Manager write-ups**: a report, three customer complaints in a day, the manager seeing you, or arriving more than 10 minutes late means a very uncomfortable talk with the manager. The third write-up gets you fired. After a talk you're left standing in the office, and the manager gives you a couple of minutes' grace.
- **Sneaking coworkers** (oddballs and blessed coworkers) face the same stakes. They use every trick you can: they grab something to hold, face onlookers with it, dash behind cover, and sit down at a desk when surrounded until it's safe to move.
- **Coworkers sit at their own desks**, so those chairs are usually taken. Sitting in someone's chair when they come back is suspicious.
- **Vision**: everyone sees 180°, but not through walls. The sight cones show exactly what they can see. Strict coworkers, customers and the manager can catch them, they get written up, and three write-ups gets them fired.
- **Blessed coworkers** never report you, but blessed friends can't help you anymore either.

## Getting around town
The city is a 4×4 grid of blocks with named streets, parks and shops, full of hedges, kiosks, phone booths, food carts, parked cars and benches. You leave and come home through your building: the elevator and the lobby (with a doorman), or the back stairs and the residents' pool, through a side gate on the street. The pool showers soak you, which makes "I was swimming" a much better excuse. Watch out for your neighbor in 7F-06, who has a habit of opening his door at exactly the wrong moment. You need to be at work by 9:00 AM. A **minimap and compass** point to your objective.
- **Weather**: sunny, cloudy or rain. Rain (and splashing cars) soaks your clothes with a wet sheen; parts you marked turn see-through. An umbrella keeps you dry.
- **Buses**: 3 lines (Red Ring, Blue Crosstown, Green Midtown). They loop on fixed routes and stop at every stop. None of them goes straight to your job.
- **Rush hour** buses are packed, so you may have to stand.
- **Boarding**: the driver always notices you, and so does everyone you pass in the aisle.
- **Sitting**: only people who walk past you or sit next to you notice. A window seat is safer than the aisle. Seats are first come, first served: you can grab one a passenger was heading for.
- **Standing**: everyone behind you can see you, so your Shame climbs steadily.
- **Days off**: run 3 errands (supermarket, pharmacy, post office, laundromat, bank, hardware store, bookstore). Watch out for chaos, like running into a coworker in town (some will tell the manager on Monday) or a dog stealing your shopping.

## VRM notes
- VRM 0.x and 1.0 files are supported (loaded with `@pixiv/three-vrm`).
- When exporting from VRoid Studio, **turn off the option that deletes hidden body parts**, otherwise the legs under the clothes may be missing.
- After importing, tick which clothing parts count as legwear (auto-detected for VRoid models). Use **Preview mishap** to check.
- If your model's body texture has no underwear, turn on "Add simple underwear overlay" (it's off by default for imported models).
- Facial expressions (happy, sad, surprised, angry, relaxed) follow how characters feel; the maximum strength is in Settings.
- Semi-transparent textures are rendered with blending; when that isn't possible they're treated as fully transparent.
- When a friend lends you their pants or skirt, their actual clothing is fitted onto your body.
- Your own character gets full hair physics. Coworkers, the manager and civilians are cheap copies of a cached model, so many can be on screen at once.
- Imported models are stored in the game's own data folder.

## Getting the .exe
- **GitHub Actions**: every push runs the *Build Windows EXE* workflow. Download `HiphtiasBlessing-windows` from the run's artifacts. Pushing a tag like `v0.2.0` also attaches the .exe to a GitHub Release.
- **Build it yourself on Windows**: install Node.js 20+, then:
  ```
  npm ci
  npm run dist:win
  ```
  The portable exe ends up in `release/`.
- **Run from source**: `npm ci` then `npm start`.

The `.exe` is portable: no installer, just double-click it. It isn't code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may warn the first time ("More info" → "Run anyway").

## Project layout
- `electron/`: desktop app wrapper
- `src/main.js`: title screen, creator, outfit screen, civilian and Celesti model managers, saving
- `src/game.js`: game loop, days and phases, player, bus riding, stealth, reactions, manager talks, the blessing
- `src/npc.js`: NPC activity queues, sight cones, carried props
- `src/bus.js`: bus lines, stops, passengers
- `src/character.js`: VRM loading and cloning, recoloring, procedural animation, built-in figures, Celesti's halo and wings
- `src/level.js`: level building, collision, sight lines, navigation
- `src/levels/`: apartment, lobby, city, workplace generator, shops
- `src/saves.js`: save slots; `src/assets.js`: bundled models in `assets/models`
- `src/minimap.js`, `src/weather.js`, `src/props.js`: minimap/compass, rain, carryable items
- `src/data.js`: jobs, personalities, tasks, errands, story text
