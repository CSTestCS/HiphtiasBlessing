# Hiphtia's Blessing

A 3D workplace stealth comedy for Windows. Import your own VRoid / VRM characters for yourself, your coworkers, your manager, the townsfolk and the angel Celesti. Then survive work, commutes and weekends after a wardrobe mishap leaves you without pants or a skirt.

## The story
Events play out differently every time: people's reactions (concern, flirting, confusion, stunned silence, laughter, disapproval), who you run into, and how the rest of your wardrobe gets ruined on the first night.

1. **Monday**: wake up, get ready and commute to work dressed normally. During one of your first four tasks (at any step of it), a wardrobe mishap that fits the task (a printer jam, a spilled pot of coffee, a dish sprayer...) takes your pants or skirt.
2. **Monday night**: you take everything down to the basement laundry room. The machine locks with all your clothes inside, your neighbor comes down to do laundry, and when the door finally opens, your whole wardrobe is ruined. From now on you go everywhere without legwear.
3. **The morning after your third shift**: you wake with a start; Celesti, the angelic messenger of **Hiphtia, goddess of hips**, appears in your apartment. You've been "blessed", and she's very sorry about it. From now on, the coworker you spend the most time near each workday gets blessed the next workday.
4. **Win** when every coworker shares the blessing.

The week runs Monday to Friday at work. Saturdays and Sundays are **days off with errands** around town.

## Main menu

The title screen shows Celesti floating in, and the main character of your last played save looking very startled about it.
- **Continue / Load Game**: multiple save slots, each its own game. Coworkers from your other saves walk around town as named townsfolk.
- **New Game**: create your character (name, gender, body, legwear, colors or your own .vrm), pick one of 7 jobs, and set up 4–12 coworkers plus a manager.
- **Settings**: length of an in-game hour (default 4 real minutes), shame buildup intensity, how fast people notice you, whether sleep reduces shame, facial expression strength, minimap on/off, bundled townsfolk on/off, and volume for master, effects, voices, ambience and interface (also in the pause menu), how see-through soaked clothes get, and how many unfinished requests are fine at clock-out.
- **Civilian Models**: 4 bundled townsperson models are used by default. Import more .vrm templates (with a gender). Every NPC gets random skin, hair, eye and clothing colors.
- **Celesti's Model**: the bundled Celesti model is used by default. Import your own .vrm (a halo and wings are added) and set her name.

Everyone starts on one of the bundled VRoid bodies (young/older, female/male), tinted with the colors you pick. Each morning an **outfit screen** lets you pick everyone's outfit for the day: which outfit, whether it's pants or a skirt today, and top/bottom color tints. You also pick your **at-home outfit** there.

Model options per outfit: which parts are legwear, which parts turn **see-through when wet**, an optional underwear overlay (off by default, since VRoid bodies already have underwear), and **wearing another model's clothes** fitted to the body.

## Jobs
Office Worker, Restaurant Server, Retail Worker, Hotel Staff, Library Assistant, Warehouse Worker and Cafe Barista. Every save generates its own workplace:
- a job-specific public floor (cubicle farm, dining room and bar, sales floor, hotel lobby, library stacks, warehouse racks, cafe)
- a back-of-house wing whose size, side and layout differ per job and per save, split into rooms and branching corridors: locker room with the time clock, manager's office, restrooms, break room, storage, loading dock, plus job rooms such as meeting rooms, kitchens, a walk-in freezer, guest rooms, a laundry, an archive, study rooms or a roastery
- lots of cover: plants, vending machines, carts, cabinets, benches, seats and hiding spots, and things to pick up and carry in front of you
- 15+ task types per job

Each coworker has a role (host, line cook, cashier, housekeeper...) with a home station and the tasks that go with it, and they pick nearby stations instead of crossing the building.

**The workday** runs 9:00 to 5:00 on the clock (speed set by the in-game hour length). Some coworkers come in late, some leave early, some stay late. At noon the break room fills up and some people head out for lunch. Work comes in as requests: coworkers and the manager walk over to ask you. If you're hiding they look around, come back later, and only leave a note after a few tries, the phone rings, orders print out. Tab switches between requests; clock out after 5:00. A security guard watches the front entrance.

**Animation.** Each item has its own grip (boxes hugged, trays carried like a waiter, bags and buckets at your side, folders against your chest, newspapers held up to read), and covering items are held low in front of you instead. Each station has its own motion: typing, writing, sorting, pressing buttons, pouring, reaching up to shelves, bending down, lifting, wiping, washing, mopping, watering, feeding the shredder, fixing things. People also talk with different gestures.

**Pushable cover**: whiteboards and carts can be pushed around (E). Keep one between you and anyone looking. Coworkers move carts around too.

**People in on the secret.** Friends who know drop by now and then to check you're okay (and can cause a distraction or lend you their pants). Oddballs who joined in come over to compare how many times each of you has been spotted. Coworkers who only see you after they've been blessed themselves have their own moment of recognition, and might team up with you; ones who saw you before apologize. After that, blessed coworkers trade a quick word when you pass.

**Dialog** is laid out like a manga panel: the speaker posed for the moment (startled, pointing, arms crossed, covering up, floating...), quotes in speech bubbles and narration in caption boxes.

**Everyone has a home.** The city has six apartment complexes. Each new game puts you in a random one and gives every coworker a home too (kept with the save). On workdays coworkers walk to work before their shift and home after it, so you can run into them on the street, with consequences. People from your other saves hang around their own buildings. Each building has its own lobby and hallway colors and name signs, and you live on a random floor. Your workplace is also placed at a random spot in the city for each new game.

**Colors.** Clothes, hair, eyes and underwear use a pool of 172 named dyes (skin tones are separate). Each color picker folds into a compact dropdown.

**Coworkers outside work.** Running into a coworker in town or on the commute never gets you reported. Ones who never saw anything at work figure you just hate wearing clothes. Ones who know have their own take. Kind coworkers who know, and fellow blessed coworkers, ask what's going on. Once you've met Celesti you can explain the blessing, which can turn them into allies.

**Kept parts.** Glasses or other accessories can be marked "Keep" (in a civilian model's Details, or in your outfit's model parts). They stay on in underwear, at home and at the pool.

**At home.** If you haven't picked an at-home outfit, you're down to your underwear at home.

**Complaints.** Customer and security complaints count per day. Anyone who has already reported you today won't report you again; they just glare.

**Morning outfit screen.** Each morning you can open the full outfit editor for anyone (model, colors, parts, underwear), the same one used at setup, and preview each outfit normally, after a mishap, soaked, or both.

**Underwear.** Every character has some: pick a design (38 cuts, 24 bras, plain ones in any color or printed sets) for yourself and your coworkers in the creator, and townsfolk get random ones. It's painted into the body texture (replacing the model's own) or, for models with a separate underwear mesh, replaces that mesh's texture.

**Civilian model details.** For imported townsperson models, *Details...* lets you set which parts take which random colors, which are legwear, which go see-through when wet, which come off at home and at the pool, and which texture holds the underwear (and bra).

**Coworkers.** Names and bodies always match the character's gender. Two days after someone is fired, a new hire takes their desk. Allies need a break between favors (about 3 minutes after a legwear swap, 2 after a distraction). Being fully soaked in see-through clothes slowly adds Shame whenever people can see you, whatever you're holding.

**Sound.** Everything is synthesized live (no audio files): footsteps that change with the floor (and other people's, so you can hear someone coming), doors, elevators, office machines, phones, buses, rain and thunder, a heartbeat that quickens as someone starts to notice you, manga-style stingers when you're caught, wordless voices for reactions (gasps, giggles, wolf whistles, tutting), and ambience for every area.

## Rules
- **Shame** rises when people outside work see you and when you're caught at work. Fill it and you lose. Sleeping lowers it. The Settings screen scales how fast it builds.
- **Makeshift skirts**: tie a carried box, newspaper, folder, tote bag, towel or tablecloth on with F. Each fails in its own way: boxes crack, paper blows away, bags tear, cloth snags on chairs. People find it odd, so it still drains Composure.
- **Excuses**: when someone comes over because you're acting strange, pick an excuse ("Posture exercise.", "Workplace ergonomics."...). Whether it works depends on who's asking.
- **Hiding**: carry a box, tray, bag, newspaper, pillow, towel... in front of you (umbrellas keep the rain off but don't hide you) (E to pick up, G to put down), sit at desks, tables and benches (nobody can tell while you're seated), duck into hiding spots, or crouch behind low furniture.
- **Composure** drains when you crouch or run in plain view at work. At zero, someone comes over to check on you.
- **Stamina** limits running.
- **Coworker personalities** when they catch you:
  - Allies run real distractions: they head somewhere away from you and make a scene, and everyone nearby walks over to look. If the manager is about to spot you, an ally may pull them away. A coworker who's playing along stays near you now and then and warns you when someone's coming.
  - *Best Friend*: becomes an ally. Takes turns lending you their legwear (90 seconds at a time, swapped somewhere private) while they sneak around in your place, or causes a distraction.
  - *Sympathetic*: becomes an ally and causes distractions.
  - *Neutral*: lets it slide once, then reports you.
  - *Strict* / *Distant*: report you.
  - *Oddball*: thinks it's a game, slips off somewhere private to take off their own bottoms, then starts sneaking around too.
  - Anyone of the opposite gender may just look away and let it slide (once a day).
- **Manager write-ups**: a report, three customer complaints in a day, the manager seeing you, or arriving more than 10 minutes late means a very uncomfortable talk with the manager. The third write-up gets you fired. After a talk you're left standing in the office, and the manager gives you a couple of minutes' grace.
- **Sneaking coworkers** (oddballs and blessed coworkers) face the same stakes. They use every trick you can: they grab something to hold, face onlookers with it, dash behind cover, and sit down at a desk when surrounded until it's safe to move.
- **Coworkers sit at their own desks**, so those chairs are usually taken. Sitting in someone's chair when they come back is suspicious.
- **Vision**: everyone sees 180°, but not through walls. The sight cones show exactly what they can see. Strict coworkers, customers and the manager can catch them, they get written up, and three write-ups gets them fired.
- **Managers** have a hidden personality that changes how they talk to you, but they always write you up. An oddball manager sneaks around too. Coworkers and customers who catch them report them to HR (you never report anyone), and after three reports they're let go. A coworker with a clean record gets promoted (their old job is filled a few days later); if everyone has a write-up, a new manager is hired.
- **Fitting rooms** (retail): shoppers change in them piece by piece and try on other colors (people from your other saves try on their other outfits). Barge into an occupied one and they complain at once.
- **Borrowing**: if a friend says no because it's too public, they walk to the nearest empty room and offer the swap there.
- **Ordinary mishaps**: very rarely, anyone's clothes give out, blessed or not. A coworker it happens to has to sneak around for the rest of the day (an oddball may decide to keep doing it); everyone else is dressed properly again the next day.
- **Customers with business in back rooms** go past the STAFF ONLY doors: hotel guests check in and go to a booked room, office clients sit in on meetings, and anyone may use the restroom.
- **Visiting coworkers**: what they have on depends on the hour. Mid-day they're dressed but barefoot, in the morning and evening barefoot without legwear, and at night in their at-home clothes.
- **Blessed coworkers** never report you, but blessed friends can't help you anymore either.

## Getting around town
The city is a 4×4 grid of blocks with named streets, parks and shops, full of hedges, kiosks, phone booths, food carts, parked cars and benches. You leave and come home through your building: the elevator and the lobby (with a doorman), or the back stairs and the residents' pool, through a side gate on the street. The pool is a real sunken pool you can wade into (it soaks you, and anyone else who goes in), and the showers soak you too, which makes "I was swimming" a much better excuse. People at the pool are in their underwear too, and nobody there questions yours. Watch out for your neighbor in 7F-06, who has a habit of opening his door at exactly the wrong moment. You need to be at work by 9:00 AM. Random outfits are color-coordinated: legwear contrasts the top's brightness, accents complement it, and shoes are mostly black or dark brown. Eye color can be set in the character editors. Outfits can mix and match: untick any of a body's own clothing parts and add single pieces (or all the clothes) from other bodies, fitted to yours without hiding the body underneath, and "Randomize colors" also rolls new underwear (style, color and print). A **minimap and compass** point to your objective.
- **Free movement**: leave home before finishing your routine, go back anytime, and carry things out with you. Take the elevator to other floors, knock on coworkers' doors (they invite you in if they're home and like you), and walk into other buildings and workplaces. Before you're dressed the usual stealth rules apply, and being seen fully undressed outside doubles the shame (the hallway and pool still pass as swimwear).
- **Weather**: sunny, cloudy or rain, and it can change during the day. Hold a carried item over your head with T to keep the rain off (it stops covering your front).
  Rain (and splashing cars) soaks your clothes with a wet sheen; parts you marked (tops on the bundled models) turn see-through. An umbrella keeps you dry.
- **Buses**: 3 lines (Red Ring, Blue Crosstown, Green Midtown). They loop on fixed routes and stop at every stop. None of them goes straight to your job.
- **Rush hour** buses are packed, so you may have to stand.
- **Boarding**: the driver always notices you, and so does everyone you pass in the aisle.
- **Sitting**: only people who walk past you or sit next to you notice. A window seat is safer than the aisle. Seats are first come, first served: you can grab one a passenger was heading for.
- **Standing**: everyone behind you can see you, so your Shame climbs steadily.
- **Days off**: run 3 errands (supermarket, pharmacy, post office, laundromat, bank, hardware store, bookstore). Watch out for chaos, like running into a coworker in town (some will tell the manager on Monday) or a dog stealing your shopping.

## VRM notes
- VRM 0.x and 1.0 files are supported (loaded with `@pixiv/three-vrm`).
- When exporting from VRoid Studio, **turn off the option that deletes hidden body parts**, otherwise the legs under the clothes may be missing.
- After importing, tick which clothing parts count as legwear (auto-detected for VRoid models). Use **Preview mishap** to check.
- If your model's body texture has no underwear, turn on "Add simple underwear overlay" (it's off by default for imported models).
- Facial expressions (happy, sad, surprised, angry, relaxed) follow how characters feel; the maximum strength is in Settings.
- Semi-transparent textures are rendered with blending; when that isn't possible they're treated as fully transparent.
- When a friend lends you their pants or skirt, their actual clothing is fitted onto your body.
- Your own character gets full hair physics. Coworkers, the manager and civilians are cheap copies of a cached model, so many can be on screen at once.
- Imported models are stored in the game's own data folder.

## Getting the .exe
- **GitHub Actions**: every push runs the *Build Windows EXE* workflow. Download `HiphtiasBlessing-windows` from the run's artifacts. Pushing a tag like `v0.2.0` also attaches the .exe to a GitHub Release.
- **Build it yourself on Windows**: install Node.js 20+, then:
  ```
  npm ci
  npm run dist:win
  ```
  The portable exe ends up in `release/`.
- **Run from source**: `npm ci` then `npm start`.

The `.exe` is portable: no installer, just double-click it. It isn't code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may warn the first time ("More info" → "Run anyway").
